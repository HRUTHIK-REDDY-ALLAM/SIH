# DUP-FIN — Duplicate financing of receivables (curated summary)

> Curated prototype summary of RBI TReDS guidelines on invoice financing and
> the rationale for a central receivables registry. Not verbatim law.

## §1 One receivable, one financier
A trade receivable may back at most one financing at a time. Financing the
same invoice with two lenders (double financing) means two parties rely on a
single buyer payment — a recognised fraud typology in trade finance.

## §2 Registry check
Before financing, the lender must check a receivables registry for an existing
lien or assignment on the invoice — by its government e-invoice identifier and
by content-based identification that survives re-issuance of the invoice
document. An active lien held by another lender is an absolute bar.

## §3 Lien lifecycle
On disbursal the financier must register its lien; on settlement the lien must
be released so the registry reflects live encumbrances only.

## §4 Circular trading
Lenders should additionally screen for circular trading structures — chains of
related-party invoices in which financing flows loop back to their origin,
inflating apparent trade volume without genuine underlying commerce.
