# FEMA-EXP — Export realisation and repatriation under FEMA (curated summary)

> Curated prototype summary of FEMA export-of-goods regulations and RBI Master
> Direction on Export of Goods and Services. Not verbatim law — swap this file
> for the authoritative text in production.

## §1 Declaration of exports
Every export of goods from India must be declared with full export value. For
electronically invoiced B2B exports, the invoice must carry a valid IRN issued
by the Invoice Registration Portal, and the declared value must match the
registered e-invoice.

## §2 Realisation period
The full export value must be realised and repatriated to India within nine
months (270 days) from the date of export. Financing tenors against an export
receivable must therefore fit inside this realisation window; a receivable due
beyond 270 days from shipment is not eligible for advance financing without
specific RBI approval.

## §3 Advance against receivables
An exporter may receive an advance against an export receivable from an
authorised dealer or licensed financier. The financier must verify that the
underlying shipment is genuine and that the receivable has not been assigned
to another lender.

## §4 Write-offs and disputes
Unrealised export proceeds require reporting; repeated non-realisation affects
the exporter's caution-listing status with the authorised dealer bank.
