# ITFS-GC — GIFT City ITFS framework for cross-border trade finance (curated summary)

> Curated prototype summary of the IFSCA framework for International Trade
> Financing Services platforms at GIFT City. Not verbatim law.

## §1 Platform model
An ITFS platform enables exporters to auction or place trade receivables with
licensed financiers (banks, NBFCs, IFSC banking units). The platform is an
intermediary; credit exposure sits with the accepting financier.

## §2 Decisioning vs lending
A decisioning service may underwrite and recommend, but disbursement must be
executed by a licensed financier under its own credit policy or a delegated
mandate. The mandate's limits (exposure caps, tenor caps, minimum score)
bind automated acceptance.

## §3 Settlement rails
Cross-border settlement between India and Singapore may use correspondent
banking or the UPI–PayNow linkage for eligible flows; the financing leg in
INR settles domestically (NEFT/RTGS) with the lien released on repayment.
