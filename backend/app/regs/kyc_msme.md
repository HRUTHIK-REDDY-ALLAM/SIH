# KYC-MSME — KYC requirements for MSME borrower onboarding (curated summary)

> Curated prototype summary of RBI KYC Master Direction as applied to MSME
> lending, including CKYC registry usage. Not verbatim law.

## §1 Identity of the borrowing entity
The lender must establish the legal identity of the MSME (certificate of
incorporation, GSTIN, IEC for exporters) and of its beneficial owners before
any credit exposure. A CKYC registry record or equivalent digital KYC
(DigiLocker-verified documents, video-KYC) satisfies the requirement.

## §2 Verified-status precondition
Credit must not be disbursed to an entity whose KYC status is incomplete or
expired. Automated underwriting may proceed to a decision, but disbursal must
gate on verified KYC.

## §3 Periodic refresh
KYC must be refreshed on a risk-based cadence; high-risk profiles require more
frequent refresh and enhanced due diligence.
