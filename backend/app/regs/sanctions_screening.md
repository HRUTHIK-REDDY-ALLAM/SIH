# SANC-SCR — Sanctions screening for cross-border trade finance (curated summary)

> Curated prototype summary of UN Security Council consolidated-list
> obligations, OFAC SDN screening practice, and MAS Notice 626 expectations
> for Singapore counterparties. Not verbatim law.

## §1 Screening obligation
Before financing a cross-border receivable, both the exporter and the foreign
buyer must be screened against applicable sanctions lists (UN consolidated
list, OFAC SDN, and — for Singapore counterparties — MAS-designated lists).
A positive match prohibits the transaction pending manual review.

## §2 Ongoing screening
Screening is point-in-time at underwriting and must be repeated at disbursal
if material time has passed. Matches, including false-positive resolutions,
must be recorded in the audit trail.

## §3 Singapore counterparty checks
For a Singapore buyer, registration status with ACRA and the entity's UEN
should be verified alongside sanctions screening; a struck-off or suspended
entity must not be treated as a valid obligor.
